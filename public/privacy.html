Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 24, 2026

DoorOps ("we", "our", or "us") provides a field inspection platform for overhead door service companies. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our application at app.doorops.app.

Information We Collect

When you use DoorOps, we collect:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and password (hashed) when you sign up
  • Company information: Company name and details you provide during registration
  • Inspection data: Property addresses, door details, deficiency notes, and reports you create
  • Usage data: Log data including IP addresses, browser type, and pages visited
  • Photos: Images you upload during inspections (stored securely on our servers)

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide and operate the DoorOps platform
  • To generate inspection reports on your behalf
  • To communicate with you about your account and service updates
  • To improve our platform based on usage patterns
  • To comply with legal obligations

Data Storage & Security

Your data is stored on secure servers in Canada. We use industry-standard encryption (TLS) for all data in transit. Passwords are hashed using bcrypt and never stored in plain text. We do not sell your data to third parties.

Multi-Tenant Isolation

DoorOps is a multi-tenant platform. Each company's data is strictly isolated — your inspection data is never accessible to other companies using DoorOps.

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You may request deletion of your account and all associated data by contacting us at privacy@doorops.app. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

Photos & Media

Photos you upload are stored securely and associated with your company account only. They are used solely to generate inspection reports and are not shared with any third parties.

Cookies

We use a single session cookie to keep you logged in. We do not use advertising cookies or third-party tracking cookies.

Third-Party Services

DoorOps may integrate with third-party services such as Jobber for job management. These integrations are governed by the respective third party's privacy policies. We only share data with third parties at your explicit request.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw consent at any time

Children's Privacy

DoorOps is a business platform intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via email or in-app notification.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact us at:
